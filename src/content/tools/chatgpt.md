## What ChatGPT is

ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI assistant for explanation, coding, analysis, writing, brainstorming, and research workflows. Use it to accelerate thinking, not replace verification.

## Best use cases

- Learn difficult technical concepts step by step
- Debug and review code
- Turn rough ideas into structured plans
- Summarize and compare information
- Create drafts that you verify and improve

## How to use it well

Give clear context, constraints, examples, and the expected output. For technical work, test important code and verify important claims against primary sources.

## Strengths and limitations

The strengths are breadth, speed, and conversational iteration. Limitations include factual errors and stale or incomplete answers. Keep human judgment in the loop.
