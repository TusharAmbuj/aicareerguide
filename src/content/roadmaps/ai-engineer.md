## How to use this roadmap

Follow the phases in order. Open a checkpoint for its details, use the linked guides when you need more depth, and build at the project checkpoints instead of waiting until the end.

## What this path optimizes for

Strong software fundamentals first, then machine learning, LLM engineering, production AI, and a portfolio that proves you can ship.

## Progress rule

You do not need to finish every optional topic before moving forward. Finish the core checkpoints, build the projects, and return to specializations when your goals require them.
