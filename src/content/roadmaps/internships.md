## How to use this roadmap

Build your fundamentals first, then create evidence, improve your resume, target roles, and practice interview fundamentals. The roadmap is a sequence of actions, not a reading list.

## What this path optimizes for

Getting internship-ready through skills, projects, applications, and interview preparation.

## Progress rule

Apply while learning. Do not wait until every checkpoint is complete.
