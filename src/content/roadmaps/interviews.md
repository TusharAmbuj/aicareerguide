## How to use this roadmap

Choose the interview type first, then practice the matching skills, patterns, and project explanations. Use the checkpoints to identify weak areas instead of trying to study everything at once.

## What this path optimizes for

Clear problem solving, strong fundamentals, and the ability to explain your engineering decisions under pressure.

## Progress rule

Use failed interviews and practice problems as feedback. Revisit weak areas deliberately.
